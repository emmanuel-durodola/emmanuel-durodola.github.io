About Me

I am a graduate student in the Physics and Astronomy department at Dartmouth College, working alongside Prof. Ryan Hickox (pictured here). I use observations from the James Webb Space Telescope to better understand black hole–galaxy coevolution in the early universe.

Research

I am broadly interested in black hole evolution. More specifically, I am interested in accretion phenomena, Active Galactic Nuclei, black hole seeding, and black hole–host galaxy coevolution.
